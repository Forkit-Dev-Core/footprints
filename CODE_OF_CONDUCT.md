# Code of conduct

People of every background, location, language, and experience level are welcome. Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Critique ideas rather than people, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and make room for newcomers.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and publication of someone else's private information are not acceptable. Do not pressure contributors to disclose personal or workplace information.

This applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and community spaces linked by maintainers.

## Reporting and response

Report concerns privately to **info@forkit.dev**, the project's existing community contact. Include only the information needed to understand the concern. Do not repeat private information in a public report.

Maintainers review reports discreetly, share details only as needed to investigate, and take proportionate action: clarification, a warning, content moderation, or temporary or permanent participation restrictions. A maintainer involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome. Ask for another maintainer to review a decision through the same private channel. No response-time or absolute confidentiality guarantee is made.
